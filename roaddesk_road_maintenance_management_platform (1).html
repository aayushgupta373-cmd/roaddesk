The Problem Statement

Damaged roads—such as potholes, cracks, and broken surfaces—cause accidents, traffic delays, and daily inconvenience. Currently, municipal authorities often receive vague complaints without exact location details or urgency levels.

“How can municipal authorities manage road-maintenance complaints and coordinate repair work in a more organized way?”

Project Objective

Provide one common web platform where road issues are reported with structured details (issue type, map location, and severity), reviewed by administrators, assigned to municipal workers, and repaired by labour personnel.

  • • Converts general complaints into actionable field data
  • • Enables severity-based prioritization for urgent safety risks
  • • Connects complaint registration directly to physical repair work

One System. One Process. (4-Step Workflow)

STEP 01

Report

Citizen submits road problem with exact map pin, category, and severity level.

STEP 02

Review

Municipal authority verifies the report details and confirms location accuracy.

STEP 03

Manage

Administrators prioritize urgent cases and assign a municipal worker & crew.

STEP 04

Repair

Workers and labour personnel complete the field repair and log completion.

Portal 01

Citizen Portal

Simple interface for citizens to report potholes, cracks, and damaged roads with pinpointed locations and severity ratings, plus track their submitted reports.

Portal 02

Admin & Municipal Worker Portal

Dashboard filtered by status and severity. Administrators review reports, update statuses, and assign field workers to coordinate maintenance.

Portal 03

Labour Portal

Dedicated field-task view for repair crews showing assigned jobs, location notes, severity priority, and one-click status updates for on-site repair work.
