Report a road issue

You need a free account so staff can follow up with you.

What a report includes

  • A pin on the mapPlaced by clicking the map or using your phone location.
  • A severity levelFrom low to critical, so urgent hazards are seen first.
  • A photoOptional, shrunk on your device before it is uploaded.
  • A short descriptionWhat is wrong and any landmark nearby.

How it works

  1. Report

    Choose the type of problem, place the pin and add a photo and description.

  2. Review

    Municipal staff verify the report, adjust the severity and assign it for repair.

  3. Track

    Each report in your account shows its current status and a history of changes.

How severity is chosen

You suggest a level when you report. Staff can change it after checking the location.

Low
Surface wear that does not affect safe travel, such as a hairline crack.
Medium
Damage that is easy to notice and may get worse, such as a shallow pothole.
High
Damage that can harm vehicles or riders, such as a deep pothole on a busy road.
Critical
Immediate danger, such as an open manhole or a collapsed road edge. If someone is at risk right now, call the emergency number (112 in India) first.

For municipal staff

Staff accounts are created by the platform administrator. Once signed in, staff can:

  • Work from one queue, filtered by severity, status and category.
  • See all open reports on a map and open any report from its pin.
  • Verify, assign and resolve reports, with every change recorded in a history.
  • View counts by status, severity, category and month.
