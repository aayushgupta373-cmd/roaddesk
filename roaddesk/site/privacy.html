Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

1. Who we are

RoadDesk is operated by {{OPERATOR_NAME}} ("we", "us"). You can contact us about privacy at {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

2. What we collect

  • Account data: your name, email address, an optional phone number and your password. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes by our authentication provider. We cannot read them.
  • Report data: the title, description, category, severity, map coordinates, optional street or landmark text and optional photo you submit, plus the time of submission and later status changes.
  • Technical data: your IP address and browser details, which our hosting and database providers record in security and server logs.
  • Your choice about analytics, stored in your browser.

3. Photos

Photos are reduced in size in your browser before upload, which also removes hidden camera metadata such as embedded GPS location. The visible content of a photo is not altered, so please avoid showing people, faces or vehicle number plates. Photo files are stored with random names and can be opened by anyone who has the exact link.

4. Why we use your data

  • To create and secure your account.
  • To let municipal staff review, verify, assign and resolve the road issues you report, and to contact you about them.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent spam and abuse.
  • If you allow it, to count visits and understand which pages are used.

5. Who can see your data

Other users cannot see your reports or profile. Municipal staff with administrator accounts can see your reports, your name and your phone number if you gave one. We do not sell your data.

We use these service providers, who process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase, for the database, sign-in and photo storage.
  • Netlify, for hosting this website.
  • OpenStreetMap tile servers, which receive your IP address and the map area you view whenever a map is shown.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile, if enabled, to detect automated sign-ups.
  • GoatCounter, only if you allow visit counts.

6. Cookies and browser storage

We store your login session and your cookie choice in your browser's local storage. These are required for the site to work. GoatCounter analytics, if you allow them, do not set cookies and do not follow you across other websites. You can change your choice at any time with "Cookie settings" in the footer.

7. How long we keep data

We keep your account and reports until you ask us to delete them or until the service is closed. Provider logs are kept for the period set by each provider.

8. Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it or delete it by writing to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. Where the laws of {{JURISDICTION}}, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, apply, you have the rights those laws give you.

9. Children

RoadDesk is not intended for children under 18. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

10. Security

The site is served over HTTPS, access to data is limited by role at the database level, and administrator pages require an administrator account. No system is completely secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

11. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above. Material changes will also be announced on this site.
